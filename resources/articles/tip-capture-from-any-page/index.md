# Start a post from the page you are reading

Drag Blowhorn: capture to your bookmarks bar once. Then select a line on any page and click it. Blowhorn opens a new post with your text, the link and the page title already filled in.


Most posts start with something you read. Copying the link, the title and the line that caught your eye into a new post takes three trips between windows, and each trip is a chance to paste the wrong thing.

The bookmarklet makes it one click. Drag **Blowhorn: capture** from the [download page](/download/) or **Settings › Setup** onto your Google Chrome bookmarks bar. On any page, select the text you want to share and click the bookmark. Blowhorn opens a new post with that text, the link and the title filled in. Pick a platform and a profile, then **Add to queue**. Nothing is queued until you do, and the bookmarklet holds no password and reaches no account.
