The Blowhorn app, screen by screen

The menu-bar popover, the console screens, Setup, Settings, and what each one does.

What each part of the Blowhorn desktop app shows and does. For step-by-step tasks, follow the how-to guides under Related.

The app at a glance

The app has two parts: a small popover that opens from the Blowhorn icon in the menu bar, and a full window called the console. The sketch below numbers the console's main areas.

  • 1. Window controls. Close, hide and resize, as in any Mac app.
  • 2. The rail. The column on the left: the places you can go, this computer's duty switch and your account button.
  • 3. The screen toolbar. The screen's tabs, filters and search.
  • 4. The main area. The screen's list, table, calendar or cards.
  • 5. Details window. Clicking a row opens its details over the screen.
  • 6. The footer. A status line at the bottom of some screens.
  • 7. The New order button. The round + in the lower right of every screen.
  • 8. The dimmed layer. The screen dims behind an open window, such as New order. It is not drawn in the sketch.
  • 9. The notice line. Appears under the toolbar only when something needs you, such as an outage.

The menu bar and the popover

From top to bottom, the popover shows:

  • The header: a ? that says what the popover is for, Settings and Quit.
  • The scheduler line: Working now while a check runs, or Last check and when it finished.
  • Three stats: items that need you, runs in the last seven days, and the success share for that week. A dash means no runs or no reading.
  • New order, or New activity when Major Blowhorn is off in Settings.
  • NEXT UP: the next three due items. open › opens one in Activity.
  • RECENTLY DONE: the three newest posts from the last seven days. ↗ opens a post's proof link in Google Chrome.
  • Duty: the This computer and Fleet switches. Each name opens its screen.
  • Open console, and an update card when a new version is ready.

A notice line appears under the header when something needs you, such as an outage. To stop everything, use the Posture screen or blowhorn stop --all.

While this computer is off duty, the OFF DUTY board replaces NEXT UP. It says what holds this computer (its own switch, the fleet, quiet hours or a stop) and lists the first waiting items. NEXT UP comes back when you go on duty again.

Drop a link, image or video

Drop something on the popover to start from it:

  • A link to a post or comment on LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Bluesky, Slack or GitHub opens the engage sheet. It offers only the verbs that platform takes: Respond, Amplify or both. Only profiles with an active account on that platform are offered.
  • Any other link, such as a Hacker News item, starts a new order from the page's title and summary.
  • An image or video starts a new order.
  • Anything else is refused with "Links, images or video only".

Respond or Amplify writes an approved item, due now, for each profile you pick. Undo stays available for 10 seconds.

New order reads the page in front from the Blowhorn extension in Google Chrome. An open post opens the engage sheet, and any other page starts a new order from that page. Without the extension, New order still opens, and any kind that needs the extension says so with Install the extension and Try again.

When an update is ready, Update on the popover's update card installs it and relaunches the app. See Update Blowhorn.

The console

The console is the full window. Open it from the popover. The rail lists five places:

  • Dashboard, which opens Home.
  • Activity
  • Profiles
  • Performance
  • Posture

Each screen reads live state when it opens. Until you are signed in and the extension is ready, every place but Home is locked. See Set up Blowhorn at first muster.

The duty switch

The switch at the bottom of the rail covers this computer only: On duty or Off duty. Press it, or press ⌘⇧P, to change it. Hover it to see the next scheduled run.

If the background service is not running, a line with a red light appears above the switch: Blowhorn service is not running on this computer. Nothing scheduled goes out until the service runs again. Click the line to open This computer, which says why and offers the fix. To take every computer off duty, see Pause and resume posting.

The account button

The account button under the switch shows your photo or initials, your name, and your organization and plan, for example "Acme · Free plan". It opens a menu with:

  • Your account and plan, which opens your account at cloud.blowhorn.ai.
  • Switch organization, when you belong to more than one.
  • Settings (⌘,).
  • Debug, which opens the Debug area.
  • Help and docs.
  • Sign out.

The menu works from the keyboard: the arrow keys move, Return chooses and Esc closes.

New order

The round + button in the lower right of every screen opens New order. You can also press ⌘N. Press and hold the button to fan out shortcuts for each kind of order and each connected platform.

An order is a form you fill in one step at a time. A finished step folds to a one-line summary, and you can press it to change it.

  1. Dispatch to. Pick one or more platforms: LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Bluesky, Slack, GitHub or YouTube. Then pick the kind of order. Compose a dispatch is a post, Answer the line is a reply to a link, and Rally the troops amplifies a link. Only kinds that every picked platform supports are offered. Or run lists one-off actions, such as Accept invitations on LinkedIn, Follow accounts on X, or Collect analytics on YouTube.
  2. From the desk of. Pick one or more profiles. Each profile sends its own copy to each platform, so two platforms from two profiles makes four posts.
  3. The message. Type the post, with a character count. Type @ to tag someone. Who's who then asks you to confirm each tag's handle on each platform. LinkedIn can't carry tags yet, and YouTube takes none, so tags go to both as plain text. A Radio net sketches a conversation between profiles, but cannot deploy yet.
  4. Landing zone. Drop or paste images or videos, or choose LZ clear for none.
  5. When to march. Choose On my mark to send now, or pick a date and time. Repeat it? turns the order into a routine. See Schedule a job.

The buttons at the bottom:

  • Post now or Schedule adds one post per platform and profile to the queue. When approval mode is all, the button reads Send for approval.
  • Dry fire rehearses the order. For each platform and profile, it opens the profile's browser, checks the sign-in and stops before composing. API-backed platforms open no browser and stop before sending. It saves nothing and ends with "Nothing was posted." Dry fire is for posts, not actions.
  • Hold in reserve saves the posts unapproved, so nothing goes out until someone approves them in Activity.
  • Cancel closes the order without saving.

After you deploy, the debrief lists each platform and when its posts go out. Debrief opens Performance filtered to this order's posts. If a platform refuses, the debrief lists each refusal with Re-engage to retry or Withdraw to drop it. What already went out is never sent twice.

Activity

Activity holds every post, routine and past run in one place.

  • List and Calendar show the same items. Calendar has day, week and month views.
  • Filters narrows by time (Past, Upcoming), recurrence (Once, Recurring), status and date range.
  • History is Activity with Past on and Upcoming off. Click a run to see what ran, how it ended and its log.

Click any item to open its details. Edits save as you make them.

  • A post shows its message, where and when it goes out, and its result once sent. Approve or Hold back sits in the footer.
  • A routine shows What, When, Rules and Log rows. Use Do it now to run it, the state pill to put it On hold, or its duty switch to hold just that routine.

Drag a routine on the calendar to move its next run. Times after the next one are dashed because they are estimates.

Profiles

Profiles lists every profile and the state of its accounts. Click a profile to open it. It has three tabs:

  • Accounts: each connected account. One that needs you reads Reconnect.
  • Browser: the Google Chrome profiles on this computer. Turn each one on or off for Blowhorn, sign in to accounts, and set up YouTube.
  • About: the name, description and who the profile is for, and Delete.

See Map Blowhorn profiles to Chrome profiles.

Home

Home has two tabs:

  • Today: whether Blowhorn is working and whether it needs you, your results, recent activity and what went wrong. The Fleet card lists every computer with its own duty switch. Needs you offers Reconnect for any account that has signed out.
  • Resources: tips, videos and the latest articles from Blowhorn.

Performance

Performance shows followers, growth and activity for each profile. It has three tabs: Activity, History and Report. It refreshes every 60 seconds, and Collect now gathers fresh numbers. See Collect and read your analytics.

This computer

Open This computer from its row on Home's Fleet card, or from the warning line above the duty switch. It shows whether this computer is on duty, the background service's state, the engine's version, and the Blowhorn extension in each Chrome profile.

When something is wrong, it offers the one fix, such as Register the service or Start the service. Its Troubleshooting tab shows the service's log. See Keep posting when the app is closed.

Posture

Posture holds every rule and limit Blowhorn keeps. Take leave for the fleet and Stop everything sit beside its tabs.

  • Rules: a slider sets how carefully Blowhorn works for everyone: Safety on (slow), Weapons tight (normal) or Weapons free (fast). A grid below sets a different choice for one platform, one profile, or one profile on one platform. A filled chip is a choice; a dashed chip is inherited. See the precedence rules below.
  • Limits: pace, daily caps, quiet hours, approval, dry fire, typing and pointer timing. Each saves as you change it.
  • Protection: what happens when a platform pushes back, what the last check held back, where your sign-ins live, and what Blowhorn will never do.

See Choose your rules of engagement and Which choice wins.

Settings

Open Settings from the account menu, or press ⌘,. Changes save as you make them. There is no Save button.

  • General: appearance, notifications, Major Blowhorn, sounds and usage sharing. See Change how the app looks.
  • Background & updates: startup, staying on duty after you quit, the update channel and uninstall.
  • Setup: guided setup and the extension card, which sets the extension mode.
  • Advanced: app paths and engine defaults.

Settings edits the same .blowhorn.yaml file the command line reads. See Settings and configuration.

Setup

Setup is a checklist the app opens on when its engine is not ready. It checks the engine, your organization connection, your Chrome profiles, and whether this computer is on duty. Each row reports ok, warn or off, with the fix that clears it. You can map Chrome profiles and register the background service right from this screen.

If you are setting up for the first time, the app opens guided setup instead. See Set up Blowhorn at first muster.

Debug area and support

Choose Debug in the account menu to open the Debug area. It holds tells checks, saved support packages, logs and the doctor's checks. See Run a diagnostics check with Tells.

Its Support card sends the team a diagnostics package without a terminal. Send diagnostics to support walks four steps:

  1. Build the package on this computer. Nothing is uploaded yet.
  2. Review the exact contents.
  3. Write the one feedback point, and choose whether the team may reply.
  4. Send the case.

When it is sent, the dialog shows your case reference. If a send fails, Try again resumes the same case. See Reviewed support packages.
